Owner Pitch Strategy

Winning Over the Founder of Sogno Coffee

Why this platform is a game-changer for their business model — solving morning congestion, boosting high-ticket pastry orders, and driving loyalty retention.

01

Pastry Pre-Order & Waste Elimination

Artisan pastries like Kunafa Pistachio and the viral Jumbo Croissant take hours to laminate and bake. Allowing app pre-orders gives the kitchen exact demand counts before 10 AM, eliminating stockouts and wastage.

02

The 3-Minute Pickup Surge (ZUS Benchmark)

Morning commuters avoid physical queues. An effortless in-app Self-Pickup flow enables patrons to order during their commute, doubling peak-hour coffee sales without crowding the counter.

03

"Sogno Club" Repeat LTV Retention

Replacing easily lost paper cards with an automated digital stamp tracker (*10 Sips to a Free Buttercream Latte*) locks in weekly customer habits and protects against competitors.

04

Enterprise Multi-Outlet Management

With branches in Kota Bharu, Bachok, Shah Alam, and Ampang, the platform is structured for centralized menu control with outlet-specific pricing and stock tracking.
