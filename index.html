Pitch notes

Why an ordering app helps Sogno

Less crowding at peak hours, better planning for pastries, and a reason for regulars to keep coming back.

01

Plan pastry production

Pre-orders for items like the Kunafa Pistachio and Jumbo Croissant tell the kitchen what to bake before the morning rush, so fewer items sell out or go to waste.

02

Faster pickup at peak hours

Customers order on the way and collect at the counter. The queue gets shorter and the barista team can take more orders in the same time.

03

Sogno Club loyalty

A digital stamp card (10 stamps for a free Buttercream Latte) replaces paper cards that get lost, and gives regulars a reason to return.

04

One menu for every outlet

Menu, prices and stock are managed in one place, with room for outlet-specific pricing across Kelantan and the Klang Valley.
